Our bakery

Morgenbrot is an artisan bakery specializing in German bread, baked fresh in San Francisco. We bring slow-fermented European bread culture and pastries to Hayes Valley, one loaf at a time.

Our breads and pastries are baked fresh daily in small batches. We believe good bread should be part of your daily life, not just a special occasion.
